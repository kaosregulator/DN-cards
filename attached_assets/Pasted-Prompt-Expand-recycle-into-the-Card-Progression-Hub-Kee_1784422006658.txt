♻️ Recycle

Recycle only the currently selected card's duplicate copies.

Duplicates are converted into Scrap.

The amount of Scrap awarded should scale with:

Card rarity
Card value
Any future event modifiers

Example:

Common low-value cards → small Scrap reward.
Rare/valuable cards → significantly more Scrap.

This makes valuable cards feel rewarding to recycle while preserving the existing rarity/value system.

🌟 Fuse All

Fuse only duplicates of the currently selected card.

Never consume unrelated cards.

Find the strongest owned copy of that card and consume all eligible duplicates to increase its level.

Ascension

Once a card reaches Level 100, it can Ascend.

Example:

☆☆☆☆☆ Level 100

↓

★☆☆☆☆ Level 1

The level resets to 1.

The star increases by one.

The card's power increases.

The player repeats this process:

☆☆☆☆☆ → ★☆☆☆☆ → ★★☆☆☆ → ★★★☆☆ → ★★★★☆ → ★★★★★

Finally:

★★★★★ Level 100

This is the true completed version of the card.

Important

Do not allow players to skip Ascension.

A player must:

Reach Level 100.
Ascend.
Return to Level 1.
Level back to 100.
Repeat.

This creates meaningful long-term progression.

Scrap Economy

Cards should recycle based on their value.

Example:

Higher rarity = more Scrap.
Higher market value = more Scrap.
Event/Limited cards can provide bonus Scrap if desired.

This keeps every duplicate useful.

Players now have meaningful choices:

♻️ Recycle unwanted cards into Scrap.
🌟 Fuse favorite cards to level them.
⬆️ Ascend max-level cards to higher stars.
Animations
